Maximum number of Nomad jobs this template may have running at once.
Leave empty or set -1 for unlimited.

Only jobs that are still pending or running count towards the limit; jobs Nomad has finished with do not, even though Nomad keeps them listed until its own garbage collection runs.

Note: the count is made by Nomad job name prefix. Two templates that share the same Prefix therefore share a single limit. Give each template its own prefix if you want their limits to be independent.
